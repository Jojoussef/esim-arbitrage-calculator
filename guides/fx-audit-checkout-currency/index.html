Currency arbitrage

Which Currency Should You Pay an eSIM In?

There is a dropdown on every eSIM checkout that lets you pick what currency you pay in. Most people never touch it. Here is what it is worth.

The result: HKD is priced 18.0% below the market rate, while ILS is priced 16.6% above it. On a $30 plan that is roughly 5.40 dollars of pure margin, every single time.

Why the prices differ at all

The vendor does not set 20 separate price lists. It sets one price in Euros and then converts that number into every local currency it sells in. That conversion is not done at the live market rate — it is done at a rate the vendor picked months ago and rounds to a tidy-looking number.

The result is a set of 20 slightly wrong rates, some of which favour the buyer and some of which punish them. Nothing about this is a promotion or a special deal. It is an accounting artefact, and it is completely free to exploit.

How to do it in 10 seconds

  1. Open the vendor checkout with the destination and duration you want.
  2. Find the currency selector. It is usually a flag icon in the header, or a currency code next to the price.
  3. Switch it to HKD.
  4. Confirm the total dropped. If it went up, you are on a different product or a different duration than you thought — go back and check.
One caveat. Some vendors apply the currency selector to the whole basket, others only to the subscription. And a few local payment methods can trigger a different price after you switch. Always confirm the final amount on the payment screen before paying.
