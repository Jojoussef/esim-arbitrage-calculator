Pricing bugs

Why Can a Longer eSIM Cost Less?

Every destination, currency and duration in the eSIM catalogue was checked. In 224 cases, the longer plan is listed at a lower total price than the shorter one. Here is what that is worth, and why the iOS app hides most of it.

The biggest one right now: in Dominican Republic (DOM), the 26-day plan costs COL$336,999.00 — while the 25-day plan costs COL$426,999.00. You get 1 more day of coverage and pay COL$90,000.00 less (21.1% off).

What a duration inversion actually is

Normally a longer plan costs more than a shorter one — that is the entire logic of a duration ladder. A duration inversion is when that breaks: the price table lists a 26-day plan at a lower total than the 25-day plan next to it.

It happens because the vendor does not price each duration independently. It anchors on a base rate, applies a duration multiplier, and then rounds the result into each local currency. When the rounding lands badly, the shorter plan rounds up past the longer one. The bug is in the rounding, not in the pricing strategy.

Why you have probably never found one

Because the vendor's iOS app does not list every duration the web checkout sells. We diffed the two catalogues: 4,509 of 6,802 plan variants (66.3%) are web-only, and the inversions cluster in exactly those rows. If you only ever buy eSIMs from the phone app, you are structurally unable to see them.

The catch

These are not stable. A duration inversion is a rounding artefact in a price table that gets re-generated. The moment the vendor re-prices the catalogue, the inverted rows can disappear — and there is no notification. If you were going to buy a plan anyway, checking the table before you pay is a two-minute job with real upside. But do not build a trip plan around a specific inverted row.

How to check in 30 seconds

  1. Open the full inversion table and search for your destination.
  2. Note the currency, the shorter duration and the longer duration.
  3. Open the web checkout (not the iOS app), set the longer duration, and switch the currency to the one in the table.
  4. Confirm the total really is lower before paying.
