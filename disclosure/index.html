Ranking is never paid for

When the calculator shows a "cheapest provider" card, that list is sorted by price and nothing else. We do not accept payment to appear in a list, to be placed first, or to be labelled as a recommendation. If a paid placement ever existed it would be labelled as an advertisement directly next to the link.

How the price data is collected

Prices come from the vendor's own published catalogue, captured on 2026-10-15, and converted to USD using the ECB euro reference rate and IBAN rates. We do not receive special pricing, hidden discounts or preferential treatment. If our affiliate relationship ever did change a price, we would not show that price. The full method is on the how we check prices page.

What we do not do

  • We do not insert fake review scores or fabricated "recommended" badges.
  • We do not edit the vendor's price to make our preferred vendor look cheaper than it is.
  • We do not claim to be independent of the vendors while accepting their commissions — this disclosure exists precisely because we are not.

Accuracy

Prices, availability and product features change constantly and are outside our control. Everything here reflects the catalogue as of 2026-10-15. Always confirm the final price on the vendor's checkout page before buying.
