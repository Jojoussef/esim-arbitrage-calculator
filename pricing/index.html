Questions

Do I need an account to use the calculator? No. There is no sign-up, no cookie and no tracking of what you search. Open it and use it.

Can I cancel? Any time. Access ends the moment you cancel, not when your billing period ends.

Is the data the same as the site? Exactly the same dataset - it is generated from one source, so a subscriber can never get data that disagrees with the page.

Do you sell my data? No. The site collects nothing, and the subscription does not change that.
