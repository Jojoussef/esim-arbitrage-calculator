Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-15

In plain English: using the price calculator sends nothing to us. No account, no cookie, no analytics, no record of which destination you looked up. If you subscribe, we store your email address and your plan so we can bill you and send you the alerts you asked for — and nothing else.

The free calculator

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers run on the calculator.
  • Your destination, trip length and currency choices stay in your browser. We never receive them.
  • The site is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which process the request in order to deliver the page. Neither is used to profile you.

If you subscribe

Subscribing is optional and only affects the Pro features. It does not change the calculator, and it does not add tracking to it.

  • What we store: your email address, your plan, and the destinations you choose to follow for alerts.
  • Where it is stored: a Cloudflare database, used only to deliver the features you paid for.
  • Who processes payments: our payment provider, a Merchant of Record. They collect the payment and handle the tax; we receive your email, your plan and the amount. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your session: stored in your own browser's local storage, so the free pages stay free of cookies. It is sent only to our API when you ask for a paid feature.
  • How long: until you cancel. If you cancel, we delete the watchlist and stop sending email. We keep the transaction record for as long as tax law requires, and no longer.
  • No selling, no advertising use: your details are never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to build an advertising profile.

To cancel, use the customer portal link in your confirmation email, or email partnerships@youssef-benatti.me. Access ends as soon as you cancel.

Third parties

The site links to eSIM vendors. Those links may be affiliate or sponsored links, which is explained on the disclosure page. Following a link takes you to that vendor, who then has their own privacy policy. We do not receive any information about what you do on their site.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email partnerships@youssef-benatti.me. If you are in the EU or the UK, the GDPR and UK GDPR apply to your subscription data, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Contact

Questions about any of this: partnerships@youssef-benatti.me.
